    What [uvw] means

    A direction is an arrow. Starting from a corner, move u cell lengths along x, v along y, w along z. The arrow from start to finish is [uvw]. Square brackets mean a direction; round brackets (hkl) mean a plane.

    Negatives and fractions

    A bar over a number means negative: [1̄10] goes back along x. Indices are always the smallest whole numbers, so ½ along x and 1 along y is written [120]. To draw one, divide by the biggest index so the arrow fits in the cell.

    Reading (hkl)

    For a plane, flip the indices: (hkl) crosses the axes at 1/h, 1/k, 1/l. A 0 means it never crosses that axis. (111) cuts all three at 1; (110) cuts x and y and runs parallel to z.

    Families and density

    <uvw> means every direction that looks the same by symmetry, like all six cube edges <100>. Linear density is atoms per length along a direction. Metals slip along their most crowded direction: <110> in FCC, <111> in BCC.
