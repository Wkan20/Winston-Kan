# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages. No build step. Shared code lives in plain `.js` files (`lattice.js`, `glad.js`, `resistivity.js`).

## Users

- **Semiconductor fab recruiters and engineers** (TSMC, GlobalFoundries, Intel and similar) skimming candidates for module, process, and metrology engineering internships. They spend a short time on the page and want to see whether this student understands real materials and process problems.
- **Classmates and peers** in materials science who may open and use the simulations to understand concepts from class and lab.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Winston Kan, a Materials Science & Engineering sophomore at RPI (Class of 2029) working in a thin films lab and aiming for the semiconductor industry. It hosts interactive simulations he built about real thin-film and semiconductor problems.

Success: a visitor leaves remembering who Winston is and what direction he's headed: a materials student who builds working simulations of the physics behind chips and thin films.

## Positioning

The projects are working, interactive simulations grounded in published physics and checked against reference values, built by a student who also works hands-on in a sputtering lab. The portfolio shows rather than claims.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from a résumé link, application, or shared URL, on desktop or phone.
- Projects live as separate pages linked from the portfolio: Substrate Plane Viewer (`visualizer.html`), GLAD Film Simulator (`glad.html`), and a resistivity size-effect explorer in progress.
- Each project has a learning notes file in the repo (`LEARNING.md`, `GLAD.md`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Everything runs client-side in the browser; no server, no accounts.
- Pages must work at phone width and in light and dark mode.
- Content is in English.
- Contact details (email, LinkedIn, GitHub username) are not yet on the site. Undecided.

## Brand Commitments

None established yet. Name: Winston Kan.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real projects with verified results: plane spacings and angles matching reference values; GLAD density and tilt trends; resistivity model matching textbook limits.
- Lab affiliation: thin films lab (sputtering) at RPI. Do not name the PI or lab on the site without Winston's confirmation.
- Prior computational work: introductory polymer chain modeling with OVITO/LAMMPS.
- No testimonials, awards, publications, or GPA on the site. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Show working things. Every project is something a visitor can use, not a description of it.
2. Accurate over impressive. Claims are checked against sources and stated with their limits.
3. Plain language. A classmate should be able to understand each project; a recruiter should grasp its point in seconds.
4. Semiconductor relevance. Projects connect materials science to real chipmaking problems.
